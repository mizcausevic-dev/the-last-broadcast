# SPOILERS: Walkthrough

Do not open this if you are playing.

## What you are proving

June Pell left on foot by the loading dock, alive, to take the real public-file copy to inspector Priya Shah. Ellis Voss prepared a substitute printout (12:06 AM) that relabels three unpaid spots as paid. Nadia's scripted caller and Cal's Tuesday pledge memo are real and are not the exit.

## Order A, note and dock

1. Booth: log, console, reel. Optional.
2. Archive: binder, inbox tray, note under the cover.
3. Dock: latch.
4. Deduction "Do the binder and the tray printout record the same facts?" Choice: they do not. Support: binder and printout.
5. Deduction "Which way out can you account for?" Choice: loading dock. Support: the note and the latch.
6. File an explanation: she left on her own, by the dock, to get the real public file to the inspector, because of Ellis's substitute file. Attach the two deductions and the note.

## Order B, witness first

1. Dock: latch, then ask Cal who used the dock.
2. Archive: binder and tray. The note can stay unread.
3. Deduction for the dock, supported by the latch and Cal's statement.
4. Deduction for the two files, supported by the binder and the printout.
5. File the same four choices. Attach the two deductions and Cal's statement about the ramp.

## Wrong on purpose

- "She is still in the station" is an incorrect account. The notebook stays.
- The four right choices with the booth log, the console, and the reel are an insufficient explanation.
- "She left through the front door," supported by the door strip and the keys, is a rejected deduction. The opposite choice with the same two entries is accepted.
